Tentang VOCS

The Voice of Computer Science, founded in 2007 and officially designated a University Choir in 2008, originated as a close-knit community of IT students united by their shared love and passion for music. What began as a small group blossomed into a harmonious ensemble, evolving into the established choir it is today. The choir not only resonates with the technical expertise of its members but also echoes the universal language of music, creating a unique and vibrant intersection of technology and artistic expression.
